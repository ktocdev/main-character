# Week 2026-W32 (2026-08-03 to 2026-08-09)

The week opened with a crash. Marcus blindsided Jordan in Monday's design review, reversing course on a direction he'd never actually communicated and tearing apart the dashboard layout in front of the team — steamrolling Lena's attempt to intervene too. Jordan held it together in the room, then went home, lied about a headache, and disappeared: phone off, two hours staring at the wall, no dinner, Pepper curling up in the way she only does when Jordan's down. Jordan named the pattern explicitly — work criticism triggers a shutdown, a withdrawal, almost a self-punishment for having cared — and noted, without illusion, that knowing the pattern doesn't stop it.

Tuesday broke the spiral, but not through any resolution with Marcus — Jordan called in sick to avoid facing him, then forced a trip to Groundwork instead of staying on the couch. Three hours into building the metronome feature for Coda, something shifted; competence returned. Mikayla's low-key check-in at the coffee shop — no advice, just company — reinforced the same lesson Jordan already half-knows: leaving the apartment and making something always lifts the fog, yet the dark-couch phase happens first every time anyway.

Midweek brought Mom's call about a Labor Day visit, which surfaced the apartment's neglected state (broken door, ceiling stain, folding table) and the lease decision Jordan's been avoiding since it came up. Jordan told her things were "fine and good," withholding the work stress to avoid worry — a small, deliberate omission that fits the week's broader theme of managing what other people get to see.

Friday's walk with Dev was the week's clearest bright spot: pre-visit nerves, Mika talking Jordan out of cancelling, then an easy afternoon where Coda came up almost involuntarily and Dev responded with real curiosity rather than obligation. The calm of being around Dev registered physically — a lowering of the anxiety frequency Jordan's been running at for weeks. Jordan is consciously not naming what this is yet.

Sunday was maintenance and avoidance in equal measure: chores done, ceiling-stain complaint filed, soup made — but the lease renewal ($150 increase, due September 15) got opened and closed without decision, and an hour of scrolling job listings produced nothing but the familiar demoralization of postings that want more than Jordan has. Going into next week: the Marcus situation is unaddressed and Monday looms again; the lease decision has a hard deadline creeping closer; Mom's visit means the apartment (and the truth) need managing; and whatever is happening with Dev remains open, undefined, and unspoken to anyone.
