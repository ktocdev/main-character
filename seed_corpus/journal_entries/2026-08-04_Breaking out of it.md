# Breaking out of it
_Date: 2026-08-04_

8/4 - called in sick. I'm not sick but I couldn't face Marcus after yesterday. woke up still feeling the Monday heaviness but forced myself out the door instead of rotting on the couch all day. went to Groundwork around 10. told myself I'd just get coffee and leave but once I sat down I opened the Coda project and something clicked. spent three hours building the practice-session timer. it has a visual metronome now - this pulsing circle that expands and contracts at whatever BPM you set. watching it work made me feel competent in a way that work hasn't in weeks.

ran into Mikayla around noon - she was picking up coffee on her way to the gym. she could tell I'd been in a funk and she didn't push, just sat with me for 15 minutes and talked about nothing. sometimes that's the best version of friendship.

by 2pm I felt like a different person than the one who crawled into bed at 9 last night. the fog just lifts once I'm out of the apartment and making something. every time. you'd think I'd learn to skip the dark-couch phase and go straight to the coffee shop but I never do.

went grocery shopping on the way home. made stir fry. texted Mika a thank-you for stopping by. she sent back a selfie from the gym looking like a maniac. love her.
