# Open mic night
_Date: 2026-08-21_

8/21 - the open mic at The Lantern. I almost backed out three times. Mika picked me up so I couldn't bail.

Dev came. they were already there when we arrived, sitting at a table near the front with a beer, looking relaxed in a way I'm constitutionally incapable of. I introduced them to Mika and they hit it off immediately - started talking about some obscure documentary about Nigerian funk music that I've never heard of but they were both deeply passionate about. I pretended to follow along.

my slot was sixth. I sat through five other acts ranging from genuinely great (a woman who played ukulele and sang something original that made me tear up) to painful (a guy who did a 9-minute spoken word piece about his electric bill). by the time they called my name my hands were shaking.

I got up there with my guitar. the spotlight was brighter than I expected. I could see Dev and Mika at the table. I started "Fast Car" and my voice was thin for the first verse. but then the chorus hit and the room was quiet - not polite quiet, actually listening quiet - and something opened up. by the second verse I wasn't thinking about the audience anymore. I was just in the song.

I didn't nail it. the bridge was rough and I lost the rhythm for a second. but I finished. people clapped. Dev whistled. Mika yelled something supportive and embarrassing. I sat down with my heart pounding and my hands still shaking and this huge grin I couldn't control.

after, we stayed for two more hours. Dev bought me a celebratory drink. Mika kept saying "I TOLD you" with this triumphant look. the three of us together felt easy and warm and like something I want to keep happening. walked home in the warm night air humming the song.
