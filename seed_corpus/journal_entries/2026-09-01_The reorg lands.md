# The reorg lands
_Date: 2026-09-01_

9/1 - the "clarity by end of month" arrived one day late, which feels about right.

I'm not being cut. internal tools is being dissolved and I'm moving to legacy marketing pages under a new manager named Priya, starting the 21st. Marcus told me in a fifteen-minute one-on-one and seemed almost pleased, like he'd gone to bat for me. maybe he did.

and I sat there and felt nothing I was supposed to feel. I kept waiting for relief to show up and instead there was this flat gray thing. legacy marketing pages is maintenance. it's updating templates for a site the company is planning to replace anyway. there are two people on it and they've both been there since the reorg before last and neither of them has been promoted in three years.

nobody transfers to legacy marketing. you get put there. it's where they park people they don't want to pay severance for yet, and everyone knows it, and Marcus said "this is a great opportunity to broaden your surface area" with a completely straight face.

so I didn't get spared. I got moved to the slow line.

I could feel the spiral loading up. the whole apparatus. go home, blinds down, phone off, don't eat, sit in it until Wednesday.

I didn't do it. I got as far as the couch, sat there about twenty minutes in the dark with my coat still on, and then got up. put the laptop in the bag and walked to Groundwork. it was 6:30 and the evening crowd was thin. got a cortado. worked on Coda's snippet library for two hours, mostly cleanup, nothing brilliant.

I want to be careful about how much I make of this because I don't want to congratulate myself for one night. but every other time it's been a full day minimum in the dark, and twice it took Mika showing up at my door. tonight it was twenty minutes and my own two legs. the middle part got shorter. I have written in here over and over that the middle part never gets shorter.

texted Lena the news. she wrote back "legacy marketing. Jordan. that's the holding pen and you know it. I start Friday. hang on."
