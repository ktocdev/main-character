# Walk with Dev
_Date: 2026-08-08_

8/8 - met Dev for coffee and a walk at Greenwood Park. I'd been nervous all morning about it, changed my shirt twice, which is embarrassing to admit. Mika had to talk me out of cancelling.

Dev is a session musician - they play bass for a few different bands and teach lessons on the side. they're easy to talk to in this way where silences don't feel awkward, they just feel like breathing. we walked the loop trail and I told them about Coda almost by accident, I was trying to describe what I do outside of work and it just came out. they actually got excited about it. asked smart questions about how the practice tracking would work, like whether it could remind you to revisit stuff you haven't touched in a while. they said they'd try it when it's ready.

we sat on the bench by the pond - the same one I was on with Mikayla last week. Dev pointed out a heron standing completely still in the shallows. we watched it for a while without talking. then they had to go teach a lesson.

I walked home the long way. it's weird how being around someone calm makes you calmer. I feel like I've been vibrating at work-anxiety frequency for weeks and this afternoon just... tuned it down. I don't know what this is yet. I'm trying not to label it too fast.
