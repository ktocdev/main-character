# Solo run clear head
_Date: 2026-08-19_

8/19 - solo run at Greenwood Park this evening. the heat broke yesterday so it was actually pleasant. did the full loop plus the hill trail extension for the first time. legs were burning on the climb but the view from the top - you can see the whole skyline - made it worth it.

while I ran I thought about the lease. it's up October 31 and I need to tell them by September 15. the rent is going up $150 which sucks. but I walked through the math: moving costs, first/last/deposit somewhere new, hiring movers or begging friends, the sheer time of apartment hunting while working. it's going to cost more to move than to stay, and the apartment is fine. the neighborhood is mine now. Groundwork is three blocks away. the park is a ten-minute jog. I know where everything is.

I'm renewing. decided it on the hill trail. feels good to close a decision that's been floating.

came home and stretched on the new green rug while Pepper batted at my shoelaces. ordered dinner from the Thai place Dev and I went to. ate on the couch watching a documentary about Motown. feeling assured this evening. the kind I want more of.
