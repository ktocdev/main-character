# Building the presentation
_Date: 2026-09-17_

9/17 - started building the portfolio presentation for the 24th. four interviews plus a presentation to the full design team. one week out.

the Meridian section wrote itself - dashboard metrics, user flow before and after, the stakeholder management section that is really a section about designing around Marcus without saying so. Ana already knows the honest version from the screen. the deck just needs to show the work.

Coda is harder. I spent two hours going back through my own entries trying to find the thread. July 28th, the first night at Groundwork. August 4th, building the timer after the crash. the napkin at the Thai place with Dev. the nine days of missing my own blind spot. I was looking for a narrative arc for a slide deck and what I found is that I've been keeping a design log without knowing it.

the thing I didn't expect: re-reading the entries changed what I think the story is. in March I would have built a deck about the features - here's the practice log, here's the metronome, here's free play. today the story is about the blind spot. about using something every day and having someone else see it clearly in forty minutes. the presentation I'm building now is one I couldn't have built three months ago, not because the work didn't exist yet but because I hadn't written my way into understanding what the work was about.

I don't know what to do with that exactly. the journal has been doing something I didn't design it to do - holding the thread while I was too busy living it to see the shape. I'm not going to say "I keep a reflective journal and it changed how I think about my own design process" in a job interview. but it's true, and that's a strange thing to owe a piece of software.

Lena texted asking if I need anything for the 24th. I said I'm good, which is the version of asking for help I'm capable of right now. she sent back a single thumbs up, no follow-up questions, which is why Lena is Lena.

I keep doing the math on what happens if I get an offer before Priya and I have had a real conversation, and then I stop because I'm doing the thing where I get attached to an outcome that hasn't happened.

the fear from the 11th is still right where I left it. a week out, I don't think it's going anywhere.
