# Rebuilding free play
_Date: 2026-09-15_

9/15 - spent most of the afternoon writing documentation for the analytics dashboard. screenshots, flow diagrams, the kind of thing nobody reads until the person who built it is gone. which may be me by October, or may not be. I'm writing it for whoever Priya assigns after the 21st, which is a person I haven't met, managed by a person I haven't met, for a site the company plans to replace.

the irony of spending my last good week at Meridian making sure my best work survives me is not lost. but the dashboard actually helps people and I'd rather it outlive my tenure than rot because nobody knows where the config lives.

went to Groundwork after. cortado, window table, laptop. finally rebuilt the free-play review screen. Dev's session on the 3rd broke something open and I've been carrying the fix around for twelve days without touching it - neutral marks instead of highlights, and the review step asks what a mark means instead of telling you. no more "your best moments" header. no more highlight reel. the mark is just a mark until you say what it is.

it took two hours and I threw out more code than I wrote. the whole trophy-case framing was load-bearing in ways I didn't expect - the export naming, the share text, the summary card that said "you played your best on Tuesday." all of it assumed the mark meant something good. ripping that assumption out was like pulling a thread and watching the sweater come apart, except the sweater was ugly and the yarn is better.

it works now. I ran a test session, marked four spots, and the review asked me about each one. it felt like talking to someone who was actually curious instead of someone already proud of you.

the waiting part isn't hard the way I thought it would be. nine days until the onsite, six until Priya. I keep expecting the spiral to start and it doesn't. maybe that's just the delay. but I also keep coming back to what I wrote on the 14th - the thing that changed isn't the situation, it's the doors. and the doors are still open.

Pepper is on the back of the couch watching the window like she's expecting someone. nobody's coming, Pep. it's just the Tuesday bird.
