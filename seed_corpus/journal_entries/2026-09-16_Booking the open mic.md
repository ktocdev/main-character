# Booking the open mic
_Date: 2026-09-16_

9/16 - went to Dev's place after work. they're three days out from the gig and running the setlist front to back, standing in the kitchen because the acoustics are better than the bedroom. I sat on the counter and listened to the whole thing twice.

they're good. I keep wanting to qualify that - good for someone who has a day job, good for a Wednesday night - but no. they're good. the transition they marked as worst on the 3rd is clean now. they played it without flinching.

I told them I'd be there on the 19th. they said "obviously" like it had already been decided, which I guess it had.

brought the guitar picks Mom left. Dad's old ones, the heavy tortoiseshell set. Dev held one up to the light and said "these are the real thing" and put it in their case like it belonged there. I didn't expect that to hit me and it did.

Mika texted while I was on the train home. she'd seen a listing for an open mic at the Fern on the 27th and sent it with just a question mark. my first thought was the usual - I'm not ready, I haven't practiced enough, I'll do it next month. I caught it this time. it's the same voice that said I didn't have enough experience for Northlight, and that wasn't true either.

I signed up before I got off the train. September 27th, the Fern, 8pm slot.

Pepper met me at the door, which she only does when she's hungry, but I'm choosing to believe it was moral support.
