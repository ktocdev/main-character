# Being honest with Dev
_Date: 2026-08-27_

8/27 - I've been sitting on the fact that I bailed on Dev Monday with a fake excuse. said I was "swamped." I wasn't swamped, I was on my couch with the blinds closed.

so today I texted them the real version. that I got bad news at work, that I shut down, that cancelling wasn't about them. I typed it and deleted it four times before sending because the honest version makes me sound fragile and the excuse version made me sound busy, and busy is a much better look.

they wrote back in about a minute: "thank you for telling me the actual thing. I would rather know you're having a bad week than think you didn't want to see me." then, a beat later, "for what it's worth I disappear too. I just do it with a bass in my hands so it looks productive."

we're getting lunch Saturday. I feel lighter in a way that's disproportionate to how small the exchange was.

here's what I keep turning over. when I withdraw, the thing I'm protecting is the impression that I'm fine. and protecting that impression is exactly what makes the withdrawal last longer, because I can't ask for anything without breaking it. Mika got around this years ago by refusing to believe me. Dev doesn't have that history yet, so if I want them in this I have to hand it over myself.

made pasta. Pepper sat on the counter watching me chop garlic like a supervisor with no notes.
