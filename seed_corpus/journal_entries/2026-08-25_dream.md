# Dream - 2026-08-25 03:15
_Date: 2026-08-25_
_Realm: dream_

the office building was sinking. not fast - slow, floor by floor, like it was being swallowed. I was on the fourth floor and I could see the waterline rising outside the windows. brown water, not clear, with papers and coffee cups floating in it.

I needed to save my drawings. I had this stack of Coda wireframes on my desk - actual paper sketches, not digital, which doesn't make sense because I work digitally. but in the dream they were the only copies and if the water got them they'd dissolve.

I grabbed the stack and ran to the elevator. pressed the button and it opened but the car was going sideways, not up or down. I got in anyway and it moved horizontally through walls, through offices I'd never seen. one room was full of instruments - guitars hanging from the ceiling, a drum kit in the center, a bass propped in the corner that looked like Dev's. another room was completely empty except for a cat that looked like Pepper but was huge, like shoulder-height, and she just stared at me as the elevator slid past.

I got out on a floor I didn't recognize. the waterline was at the windows here too. I tried the stairs but they spiraled down into the water. I could still hear the typing from the other dream, the fast mechanical typing with nobody there.

the last thing before I woke up: I looked down and the drawings in my hands had already dissolved. my hands were wet. Pepper - real Pepper, normal-sized - was licking my fingers.

woke up at 3:15am. sat up in the dark for a while.
