# Mom called about visiting
_Date: 2026-08-07_

8/7 - Mom called tonight. she wants to come visit for Labor Day weekend, which is sweet but immediately made me look around my apartment with her eyes. the bedroom door doesn't close all the way, there's a stain on the ceiling from the upstairs neighbor's leak that maintenance never fixed, and my "dining table" is a folding card table with a plant on it.

she asked if I'd thought about renewing the lease since it's up in October. I said I hadn't decided, which is a lie - I've been avoiding the decision. it's not a bad apartment. it's just small and a little sad and the rent went up. but moving is expensive and exhausting and the idea of packing everything while also dealing with the reorg makes me want to crawl under the covers.

she told me about Dad's knees and her book club and the neighbor's dog that keeps getting into her garden. normal Mom stuff. I didn't tell her about the work stuff because she'd worry and then call me every day. I just said things are good, which isn't technically a lie - things are fine. fine and good are close enough.

Pepper sat on the kitchen counter for the entire call staring at me. she's not allowed up there. we both know it. neither of us cares.
