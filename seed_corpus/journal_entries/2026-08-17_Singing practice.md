# Singing practice
_Date: 2026-08-17_

8/17 - Mika invited me to an open mic at The Lantern on Friday. she does this thing where she drops invitations casually like "oh I signed us both up, hope that's cool" and then I'm committed before I've processed it. part of me is terrified and part of me knows she does this because left to my own devices I'd never sign up for anything.

so I've been practicing. I picked "Fast Car" because the range works for me and it's one of those songs everybody knows so even if I'm shaky the room carries it. I set up in the living room with my guitar and ran through it five times. the first two were bad - voice cracking on the higher notes, timing off on the bridge. by the third time I found a groove. by the fifth I was actually singing, not just performing the motions of singing.

Pepper sat on the amp the entire time. during the quiet verse she started howling along - not complaining, more like she was harmonizing in her own weird cat frequency. I laughed so hard I lost the chord progression.

it felt good. physical and present and not about work or screens or anxiety. just me and a guitar and a cat who thinks she can sing. I should do this more. I always forget that I like it until I do it.
