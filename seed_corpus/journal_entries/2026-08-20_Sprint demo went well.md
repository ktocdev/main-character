# Sprint demo went well
_Date: 2026-08-20_

8/20 - presented the dashboard iteration and Marcus actually liked it. or at least he didn't tear it apart. he said "this is the right direction" which from him is practically a standing ovation. Lena gave me a thumbs up from across the table. I felt like I could breathe for the first time in weeks.

the difference was that I stopped trying to guess what Marcus wanted and just designed what I thought was right. simpler nav, bigger data cards, less chrome. it's not revolutionary but it's clean and it works. sometimes the best design is the one that doesn't make you think about the design.

went to Groundwork after work to celebrate with a fancy oat milk latte and a pastry. set up at the window table and worked on Coda for two hours. got the quick-start cards hooked up to actual stored data - your last three sessions, tap to resume. watching it pull in my test data and display it correctly felt like a tiny miracle.

Lena texted me while I was there: "you crushed it today, just wanted you to know." I don't think she realizes how much that meant. it's one thing to not get criticized. it's another to have someone actually say it out loud.

came home feeling like a person who has their life approximately together. which is probably the best I'm ever going to get and honestly it's enough.
