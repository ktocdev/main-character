# Dinner with Dev
_Date: 2026-08-15_

8/15 - second time seeing Dev. dinner at that Thai place on Elm. I got there early and almost left because the anxiety kicked in - what if I'm boring, what if there's nothing to talk about, what if I made up the connection and it was just the park being pretty.

but then Dev showed up and they were wearing this old Talking Heads t-shirt and I immediately felt fine. we talked for two hours straight. they played me a song they'd been working on - pulled out their phone and held it between us at the table, both leaning in. it was really good, like genuinely. a bass-driven instrumental thing with these weird time signature changes. I said it reminded me of Thundercat and they said that was the highest possible compliment.

Dev asked about progress with Coda and I showed them the new wireframes on my phone. they scrolled through slowly and asked "can it track when I noodle? like, unstructured practice - I just play for an hour and I don't want to log it piece by piece." which is exactly the feature I haven't built yet and exactly the one I should. we sketched ideas on a napkin. a "free play" mode with just a timer and optional tags after. simple. good.

we split the check even though they tried to grab it. walked a few blocks together before heading different directions. they texted me a bass cover of a Radiohead song when they got home with no context. I like this person. I'm trying not to overthink it.
