# The holding pen and a door
_Date: 2026-09-09_

9/9 - Lena texted at 9:40am: "submitted. product designer, Northlight. talk to Ana, she runs design. told her you're the person who fixed our whole dashboard while being managed by a weathervane."

so that's real now.

spent the evening on the application. and here is the thing I did not expect - the portfolio was not the problem. I've been dreading the portfolio update since July, treating it as this enormous unstarted mountain. but I opened it and the Meridian work is fine, it's solid, it's three case studies of internal tools that measurably worked.

the piece that made it good was Coda. I wrote it up properly for the first time: the problem, the practice log, the metronome, quick-start cards, and then the free-play mode with the whole story of where it came from - Dev asking "can it track when I noodle," the timestamp idea, and then Thursday, when Dev used it as a bug tracker while I'd built it as a trophy case. I wrote that one up honestly: I dogfooded the feature daily for nine days and could not see it, because I was the wrong user and didn't know it. it's the strongest thing in the whole portfolio and it's the thing I built at a coffee shop to feel better on bad days.

Mika said this on July 31st. "maybe Coda IS the portfolio update." Lena said it on the 14th. I nodded at both of them and did not believe it. it took me writing the case study to see they were just correct.

submitted at 11:20pm. cover letter is short. I didn't do the thing where I pretend I'm passionate about their mission statement.

I'm not going to spiral about this. I'm going to go to bed. Pepper is already asleep on the pillow I was planning to use, so I'll take the other one, as is our arrangement.
