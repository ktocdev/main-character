# The holding pen and a door
_Date: 2026-09-11_

9/11 - phone screen with Ana at Northlight, 45 minutes, took it from my apartment on my lunch break with the door shut like I was doing something illegal.

it went well. I think it went well. I've been wrong about this before.

she asked about the dashboard work and I gave the honest version instead of the polished one - that the first two rounds failed because I was designing at a moving target, and the third one worked because I stopped guessing what my manager wanted and designed what the users needed. she laughed at "moving target" in a way that suggested she has met a Marcus.

then she asked what I build when nobody's assigning it, and I talked about Coda for eleven minutes. she pulled up the case study while we were on the phone. she stopped on the free-play section and asked about the trophy-case thing - specifically how long I'd been using the feature myself before that session. I said nine days, daily, and I still didn't see it. she said "good, I wanted to know if you'd pretend you had." then she said most portfolios she reads describe user research as something that confirmed the designer was right.

she wants me to come in for the onsite in about two weeks. it's a full day - four interviews back to back plus a portfolio presentation to the design team. she said "I'll be honest, we're moving fast, we need someone by October." that gave me goosebumps.

went for a run at Greenwood Park after work to burn off the adrenaline. did the hill trail. from the top I had this thought that I want to write down before I talk myself out of it: ten days ago the reorg landing was the whole sky. tonight it's an inconvenience I might be leaving. the situation didn't change at all - I still report to Priya on the 21st, I'm still headed for legacy marketing. what changed is that there's a door.

didn't tell anyone at work obviously. told Lena, Mika, Dev, and Mom, in that order, within about twenty minutes.

please never say that's not nothing

I don't want to get attached to this. I've been wrong about interviews before and the crash after is worse than the wait.
