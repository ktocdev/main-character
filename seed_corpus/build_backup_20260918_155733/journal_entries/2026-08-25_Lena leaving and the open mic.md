# Lena leaving and the open mic
_Date: 2026-08-25_

8/25 - Tuesday evening at Groundwork. I've been coming here after work so consistently that the barista just starts making my cortado when I walk in. that's either a sign of healthy routine or a sign I need to expand my world. I'm choosing to read it as the first one.

had a breakthrough on Coda. I've been struggling with how to handle the "free play" mode Dev suggested. logging unstructured practice felt like it either needed to be too complex (tag everything, categorize, rate your session) or too simple (just a timer, no data). the answer came to me while I was stirring my coffee: you record the whole session, and as you play you can tap a button to drop a timestamp at any moment that feels important. after you stop, you go back through the timestamps and add notes to each one - what you were doing, what clicked, what didn't. then you can clip snippets from those marked moments and categorize them however you want. scales, improv ideas, riffs to revisit, whatever. so you end up with this organized library of your own best moments from unstructured practice.

I built the prototype in about an hour and a half. it's rough but it works. a big START button, a running timer with the timestamp marker, and the snippet screen after. Dev is going to love it.

Mika stopped by on her way home from a run. she wanted to plan another open mic and I said yes without hesitating, which surprised both of us. she said I seem different lately. I think she means better.

walked home feeling like the work thing, whatever happens, isn't the whole story. there's Coda and Dev and Mika and music and Pepper and this apartment I decided to keep and the park three blocks away. the job is one thread. a stressful one, yeah. but it's not the whole fabric. I keep forgetting that and then remembering it at this coffee shop.
