# Lena leaving and the open mic
_Date: 2026-08-14_

8/14 - writing this early because I can't sleep. yesterday (Thursday) was a normal enough day at work. Marcus was out so the standup was just Lena and the devs, and we actually had a productive conversation about the nav component without anyone overriding anyone. Lena showed me a prototype she'd been working on quietly and it was genuinely good. I told her so and she looked surprised, like she wasn't used to hearing it.

then last night I had this dream. I walked into the office and every desk was cleared. no monitors, no keyboards, no coffee cups. the overhead lights were that harsh fluorescent but flickering. I could hear typing - fast, mechanical typing coming from everywhere - but there was nobody there. I walked through the rows calling people's names. Lena. Marcus. nobody answered. I found my desk and there was a single sticky note on the monitor that said "we moved" but no address, no floor number, nothing.

I tried to leave but the elevator was gone. just a smooth wall where the doors should be. I started looking for stairs and woke up.

woke up at 4am with my heart going. Pepper was staring at me from the foot of the bed. couldn't fall back asleep so I went for an early run at Greenwood Park. barely anyone there. the air was cool and the trail was quiet and it helped. but the dream is still stuck with me. I think the restructuring is getting to me more than I've been admitting.

8/14 - Lena took me to lunch today. she wanted to tell me in person that she accepted an offer somewhere else. she starts in three weeks.

I'm happy for her. she's been miserable for months and she deserves better than Marcus's whiplash feedback. but sitting across from her at the sandwich place I felt this sinking feeling, like one more anchor lifting. she's the person I debrief with after bad meetings. she's the one who texts "you crushed it" after demos. without her I'm just alone in that team with Marcus and people who don't push back.

she said she'd stayed longer than she should have because of me, which was kind and also made me feel guilty, like maybe I should be further along in my own exit plan. I told her I'm working on it. she said "Coda could be the thing" which is the second person to say that in two weeks.

after lunch I went back to my desk and everything felt different. same screens, same Slack channels, same Marcus silence. but knowing Lena's leaving made the walls feel thinner. like this place is temporary and I've been pretending it's permanent.

went for a short walk at Greenwood Park on the way home just to not go straight back to the apartment. sat on the bench and watched joggers go by. at least I got that early run in this morning.

the thing I can't say out loud at work is that I'm not sad she's leaving, I'm scared. those feel like they should be the same and they aren't.

three weeks. that's the part. three weeks and then the floor is just Marcus and people I nod at.
