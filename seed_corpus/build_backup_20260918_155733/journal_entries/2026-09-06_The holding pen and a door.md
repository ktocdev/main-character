# The holding pen and a door
_Date: 2026-09-06_

9/6 - Mom got in Friday night for Labor Day weekend. I cleaned for three hours beforehand and the ceiling stain is still there because maintenance did exactly what I predicted, which is nothing.

she did the tour. said the green rug "really warms it up," which is the exact thing I thought when I bought it and I felt a bit embarrassed that my mom and I share taste in decor. she brought a folding chair from home in her trunk, unprompted, because I'd mentioned once in July that I only have two. she also brought a box of Dad's old guitar picks that she found cleaning the garage. he doesn't play anymore because of the arthritis. she said "he wanted you to have them."

we walked the loop at Greenwood Park Saturday morning. and somewhere near the pond I told her about work. all of it - the restructuring, the role under evaluation, legacy marketing, Lena leaving. I'd been holding it since July specifically so she wouldn't worry.

she was quiet for a bit and then said "I wish you'd told me in July." not angry. just sad about it. she said "You can tell me anything, I'm here for you." then she asked about eight follow-up questions, several of which were sharper than anything I'd asked myself, including "what would you be doing if the job wasn't taking up so much of your mental real estate?"

I made the roasted chicken thing Saturday night and she ate at the card table without one word about the card table. Pepper, who usually hides, slept on Mom's lap for an hour.

she left this afternoon. the apartment is very quiet. I keep thinking about the July thing - that I ran a five-week information blackout on someone who would have helped. I do this. Mika, Dev, Mom. same move every time.

you're right, I skipped it. what I'd be doing is Coda, or something like it - building one small thing all the way through instead of in ninety-minute pieces at a coffee shop. and playing more. the open mic was two weeks ago, I said yes to a second one, and I have not booked it.

she also asked why I didn't tell her sooner and I said I didn't want her to worry, which is true and also not the reason.
