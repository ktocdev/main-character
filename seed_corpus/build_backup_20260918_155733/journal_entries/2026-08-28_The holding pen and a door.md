# The holding pen and a door
_Date: 2026-08-28_

8/28 - Lena's last day at Meridian. they did the thing where someone orders a sheet cake and everyone stands around the kitchen for eleven minutes and then goes back to their desks. Marcus said "we'll miss you" in the same voice he uses to accept a meeting invite.

I walked her out to the parking garage with the box of her stuff. she had that one plant she'd kept alive for two years and a mug that said WORLD'S OKAYEST DESIGNER. she was doing the bright brittle thing people do when they're trying not to make it a moment.

then at her car she said "listen. I start at Northlight next Friday, the 4th. give me a few days to get my badge and find the bathroom, and then I'm putting your name in. I mean it. their design team is four people and they're hiring, and they actually ship things." and what came out of my mouth was "really? I don't think I'd have good enough experience to work there."

she just looked at me over the roof of her car. she said "of course you do. you rebuilt the dashboard three times with a manager giving you opposite feedback every round and it shipped and people use it. that's not junior. that's the whole job." and then, because she's Lena, "I'm not doing you a favor. I'm telling Ana about someone good."

I said okay.

driving home I kept replaying how fast the not-good-enough thing came out. it wasn't modesty, I didn't decide to say it. it was just sitting there loaded. and it's not even a belief I hold when I look at it directly - I know the dashboard work was good, I wrote in here on the 20th that it was good. but the second someone offers me a door, some other part of me gets to the microphone first and explains why I shouldn't walk through it.

the floor is going to be so quiet on Monday. she was the only person I could look at during a bad meeting and have the look mean something.

she did. it went in the box on top of the mug and she made me hold it while she got her car door open.

one more thing - I've been telling myself I'm not ready to move for about a year now. I'd like to know when I started saying that.
