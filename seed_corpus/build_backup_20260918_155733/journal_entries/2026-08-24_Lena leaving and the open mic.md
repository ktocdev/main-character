# Lena leaving and the open mic
_Date: 2026-08-24_

8/24 - and there it is again. the restructuring details came out. my role is "under evaluation" which is corporate for "we might not need you." Marcus delivered it in a one-on-one with the emotional range of someone reading a bus schedule. he said there would be "clarity by end of month" and that I should "continue executing in the meantime." executing. like I'm a program.

I went back to my desk and packed up my bag and left at 4pm. didn't tell anyone. walked home in a daze. sat on the couch. the blinds were closed from this morning and I didn't open them.

cancelled on Dev. we were supposed to get lunch tomorrow but I texted some excuse about being busy. they said no worries. I feel bad about it but I also can't be around anyone right now.

Pepper climbed into my lap around 6pm. she purred so loud it vibrated my sternum. I stayed there for an hour just existing with her weight on my legs.

around 8 I texted Mika. not to talk about it, just "hey." she showed up 40 minutes later with pad thai and didn't ask any questions. we sat on the floor eating out of the containers and she told me about how after the open mic three people came up to her asking who I was. she said "you have fans, Jordan" and I laughed even though my eyes were wet.

she stayed until 10. after she left I felt the gears unsticking a little. not fixed. just mobile again. the pattern is work knocks me down → I withdraw → I need someone to come in from outside and pull me back. Mika is that person. she always has been.

I should go to Groundwork tomorrow.
