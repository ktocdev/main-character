# Groundwork nights and the reorg news
_Date: 2026-07-28_

7/28 - went to Groundwork after work with the laptop. Pepper was waiting by the door when I got home to grab the charger, doing that thing where she sits on my shoes. I almost caved and stayed in but I know if I work from the apartment I just end up on the couch refreshing feeds.

got a cortado and set up at the window table. spent about two hours on Coda's practice log screen. the idea is you log what you practiced - scales, a song section, improv - and it tracks streaks and shows you what you've been neglecting. like a fitness tracker but for music. I've been noodling on this for months and today the UI actually started feeling like something real. got the streak counter working with little animated flames. dumb but satisfying.

there's something about Groundwork that makes me productive. the noise level is right, nobody talks to me, the coffee is good. I need to come here more after work instead of pretending I'll focus at home.

came back around 8. Pepper immediately forgave me. made pasta. quiet evening. the kind of day where nothing bad happens and you realize that's actually rare enough to notice.
