# The holding pen and a door
_Date: 2026-09-14_

9/14 - signed the lease renewal tonight, one day before the deadline, which for me counts as early. one year, $150 more, through October 31st of next year. hit send at the window table at Groundwork with a cortado going cold next to the laptop, which is the correct place to have done it.

there's an argument that this is the wrong week to lock in a year. if Northlight happens, the commute changes significantly and I may need a car. but I considered it back in August and decided moving is too annoying, and I like it here. the neighborhood is mine. I could afford a car with the prospective pay increase and security of the new role. either way I'm not going to let a job I don't have yet make my housing decisions.

the other thing. I had the office dream again Friday night - third time. same empty floor, same fluorescent flicker, same mechanical typing coming from nowhere. but this time I found the stairs. they were where they've always actually been, past the kitchen, and I just walked down them and out. no water, no sideways elevator. I woke up at a normal hour and lay there for a while feeling like my own brain had handed me something slightly on the nose.

I'm not going to pretend a dream means anything causal. but I've had three dreams about being trapped in that building and the first two ended with no exit and this one didn't.

onsite interview is the 24th. Priya starts managing me on the 21st, so I'll have exactly three days of a manager I've never met before I take a personal day to interview elsewhere. the timing is cringey.

what I keep coming back to: none of this got fixed by the job resolving. the job resolved badly - I got parked in the holding pen - and I'm fine anyway. what actually changed is that I started handing people the heavy thing instead of holding it - Dev on the 27th, Mom at the pond, Lena in the parking garage saying let me do this. every single good thing in the last three weeks came in through a door I opened by telling someone the truth. I have written "I don't want help, I want to disappear" in this journal more than once. it's still true when I'm in it. it's just no longer the last word.

Pepper is sleeping on the green rug with her white feetsies curled blissfully. I'm going to bed.
