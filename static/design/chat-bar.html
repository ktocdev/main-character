Chat bar

Ask's compact composer: one --bg-muted box, a two-line textarea on top and a row of small controls under it. Smart replies sits on the left; clear and send sit on the right. It's the search bar's sibling: the same box, the same focus colour, stacked rather than in a line.

States

sending is the moment between Enter and the reply: send is disabled until the answer starts. Smart replies is a solid toggle, hidden in the demo where replies are canned. Clear is a text button.
