Disclosure

A section that opens and closes: a native <details> whose header is the section's italic accent title, with a small › that turns to ⌄ when open. A run of them, hairline between each, is help's accordion. Native, so it opens from the keyboard and for find-in-page with nothing extra.

Anatomy
details.disclosure[open]
  summary
    span.mark          › closed, ⌄ open (::before)
    span               the title
  div.disclosure-body  .8rem gap, indented 1.5rem
header
The body serif, italic, 1.3rem, --text-accent; 1.1rem above and below. The whole line is the target.
mark
UI face .8rem in --text-muted, at least .8rem wide so the title doesn't shift when it changes.
body
A column, .8rem between blocks, indented 1.5rem so it hangs under the title, with 1.5rem below.
rule
A --border-default hairline under every section, open or closed.
Tokens

Title --text-accent; mark --text-muted; rule --border-default; type --font-body, --font-ui. In Figma: Disclosure, open, with title and body properties.
